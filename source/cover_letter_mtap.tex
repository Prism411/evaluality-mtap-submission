\bigskip
\noindent \textbf{Re: Submission of ``Do Visual-Selection Operations Track Technical
Quality? A Controlled Audit for Multi-Video Summarization''}

\bigskip

Dear Editor,

We submit the manuscript above for consideration as a regular paper in
\emph{Multimedia Tools and Applications}.

Video summarization systems routinely score frames with operations borrowed from
image quality work, and those scores are then read as evidence of technical
fidelity. The manuscript asks whether they support that reading. We evaluate
\fontesDaLiteratura{} literature-derived operations from \fontesTrabalhos{} source
works on \parConstruidos{} original-versus-transformed comparisons and on
\nBases{} public image-quality databases, alongside attribute configurations and
dedicated no-reference models, and we trace the consequence through to
representative selection over \disClusters{} redundancy clusters. Every operation
with a declared direction reverses on at least one transformation type, none meets
both external screening conditions, and \resElegiveis{} of \invModelos{} dedicated
models do. One audited operation is decomposed to show the mechanism behind its
behaviour rather than only its failure rate.

We believe the work fits the journal because it is an empirical audit of components
that multimedia systems already deploy, not a proposal for a new scoring method. Its
product is a screening protocol and a reusable set of controls, with every reported
number emitted from released scripts.

\textbf{One point of transparency, stated in the manuscript and repeated here.} The
modular summarization pipeline used as testbed and the broadcast corpus it runs on
were both developed at our own laboratory, which is why we had access to the
released code and to the material. Section~3.2 states this where the testbed is
introduced, together with the reason the pipeline was chosen, namely that its
quality criterion is a replaceable component. The audited visual-word operation is
one of the works under study rather than a baseline of our own, and a negative
result for it is not a failure of its source against its own stated objective, which
was relevance and not technical fidelity. The full corpus remains with its authors and is not redistributed. The
reproducibility archive contains scripts and derived data, plus a one-topic,
three-video sample as described in the manuscript.

The manuscript is original, has not been published elsewhere, and is not under
consideration by another journal. In the interest of transparency: an earlier
submission of this work to another journal was returned at initial editorial
screening, without peer review. Analysis scripts and machine-readable results
accompany the submission as Online Resource~2, and the supplementary tables and
figures are submitted as Online Resource~1.

We thank you for your consideration.

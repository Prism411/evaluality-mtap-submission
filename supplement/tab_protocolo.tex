\rowcolor{rowlight}\textbf{Patterns} & One specific expression per operation family, never a bare word, so that \emph{contrast} alone does not match. Reference lists are cut before matching; a cut leaving under 40\,\% of the PDF is refused and the work enters whole. \textbf{Instrument accuracy} & Measured against a manual reading of the sources. \textbf{The bias runs in favour of the instrument}: the same author did the reading and the inventory, so recall is an upper estimate. \rowcolor{rowlight}\textbf{Screening criterion} & An operation enters only if its source declares a direction for its own statistic and scores a single frame. \textbf{No operation without a declared direction was screened in.} The 3 that declare a direction and stay out are sequence-level: \texttt{qaroush\_movimento}, \texttt{qaroush\_ses}, \texttt{sobel\_cv}. & 15 of 24 mapped operations \\
\textbf{Coverage achieved} & Share of the operation mentions in the archive that the screened set covers. A mention is not an article: one PDF may name several. & 41 of 71 mentions (57.7\,\%) \\
\rowcolor{rowlight}\textbf{What is left out, and why} & \textbf{Of the 30 mentions outside, 25 are not screenable by any rule}, because the source declares no direction (14) or gives no combination rule (11). That residue is under-specification in the sources, and it is the same property that would stop an independent reader from reimplementing them. & 25 of 30 not screenable \\
\textbf{What the archive does instead} & Works citing no dedicated no-reference quality model, and how many of those compute one of the audited operations in its place. \textbf{A floor}: a work that computes an operation without naming it is not counted. & 173 of 186 cite none; 33 of those use an operation \\

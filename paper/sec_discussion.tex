\section{Discussion and limitations}\label{sec:discussion}

\textbf{Taken together, the results connect provenance to behavior.} Operations that agree with one another barely above chance move from heterogeneous
source-defined roles into a common technical-quality interpretation; that interpretation
fails under controlled degradation; a count-dominance mechanism explains the deployed case,
which sits at the median of its own class; and changing the operation changes which
representatives the pipeline selects. The rest of this section develops those four links.

The first link is documented in Section~\ref{sec:prov} and the second in
Section~\ref{sec:class}. For the third, \textbf{the measurements establish count dominance}
across the evaluated configurations; on the 800
constructed pairs the signs of the score and count differences agree in \mecConcordam{}. The count
increases observed under noise and JPEG accompany the score inversions, linking the
algebraic decomposition to the measured behavior.

Across the other fifteen operations \textbf{failures under white noise are frequent},
although their patterns and exceptions do not establish a common mechanism. Figure~S2 of the supplement orders the eight
degradations by what they do to local high-frequency energy, measured on the same frames
as the median ratio to the original. The seven that reduce that energy are answered
correctly, from darkening at $84.3$ down to desaturation at $61.8$. \textbf{White noise is the only one that raises it}, by a factor of $6.9$, and it is the
one the class answers at $19.1$ per cent, with a median of $3.0$ and thirteen of the fifteen operations below
chance.


Two operations, hue count and edge distribution, answer white noise correctly at $100$ per
cent and fail elsewhere instead. JPEG, which one might expect to add structure through
block edges, in fact reduces the measured energy at a ratio of $0.95$, and is answered at
$62.9$. \emph{Structure}, \emph{descriptor count} and \emph{high-frequency energy} are
three different quantities, and only the first two were tied together by measurement.
\textbf{These differences indicate recurring degradation-specific failures} rather than a
single mechanism shared by all the evaluated operations.

\textbf{Read across the works, the audit says three things about published practice.} The
same computation carries different requirements: \citet{cakmak2025tripss} uses Laplacian
variance as a threshold that discards blurred frames, \citet{korolkov2025keyframe} as the
dominant weighted term of a ranking score. This bench measured the statistic as a scorer, so it
speaks to the second use directly and to the first only through the statistic the filter
reads. \textbf{An aggregate can also hide where a quality score would be used}: the
visual-attention module of \citet{qaroush2025diversity} averages \vaModuloOito{} per cent
over the full battery and sits at \vaRuido{} under noise and \vaJpeg{} under JPEG, the two
degradations on which such a score would reject material. And \textbf{combining terms is not by itself evidence of
robustness}: the one composite that clears sensitivity fails transfer, so a work that
publishes a combination inherits the transfer question its members never answered.

\textbf{For the technical-quality requirement evaluated here, the eligible no-reference
models are better-supported replacement candidates} than normalizing the deployed operation
by segment length or inserting a newer retrieval descriptor. LIQE, QualiCLIP and
CLIP-IQA$+$ meet the common rule and run substantially faster than the deployed path. The complete operation changes \casCompleta{} decisions, and holding the scorer fixed
while changing only the sampled frames and pooling already changes \casSoCasamento{}; an
exact paired test separates the two ($p=\casP{}$), so both scorer identity and an
operation's own input choices carry part of the effect.

\subsection{Limitations}\label{sec:limits}

Every result above is a statement about one operation, in one reading, under one battery,
and none is a verdict on a published system.

\textbf{Object and transfer.} What the bench measured is named beside each row: a term
isolated from a joint objective, a module reproduced as published, or an adaptation of
ours. A term that fails as a quality score has not thereby been shown to fail at the job
its source gives it, and count dominance is established for the deployed aggregation in the
configurations evaluated, not for every vocabulary, normalization or extractor.

\textbf{Application.} The 42 clusters are nested in only 19 topics, and both
DOVER-technical intervals cross zero: the positive raw, range and rank point estimates,
including after removing small-range clusters, do not confirm a population-level output
effect. Cluster membership means the pipeline treats segments as redundant alternatives,
not that they carry identical completeness, narrative utility, audio quality or semantic
relevance, so technical quality remains a tie-breaker rather than an objective for the summary as a
whole. \textbf{No human judged these segments}, so what the substitution measures is how far the
choice of operation moves the selection, not which selection is preferred.

\textbf{References and coverage.} KADID and the broadcast construction test responses to
degradation, not preference between distinct recordings. Q-Align's 98.2\% constructed-pair
accuracy and the 97.6\% of the exact DOVER technical output validate their response to
those constructed changes, not their use as human ground truth or as compositional
preference models; the official standardized fusion and DOVER++ remain outside the
analysis, and DOVER does not isolate temporal modeling because architecture and training
differ along with it. The screening rule admits LIQE, QualiCLIP and CLIP-IQA$+$ without
ordering them, so the LIQE substitution demonstrates propagation and not superiority.
ARNIQA and TReS are reported on completed axes only and do not enter the complete-case
claim, MANIQA is absent, and later models may change the eligible set.

\textbf{Procedure and reproduction.} \textbf{The screening rule is operational and was not
preregistered}, and the historical inference environment was not preserved as a fully
pinned lockfile, so the archive reproduces the reported statistics without claiming
bit-identical regeneration of every neural score. The diagnostic controls are descriptive:
the KonIQ--CLIVE--KADID contrasts change content, capture, score distributions and model
exposure together, and conditioning on keypoint density may remove legitimate quality
signal. Tables S15 and S16 retain those diagnostics; the main conclusion rests on the exact
factorization, the dependency-aware external measurements, the constructed pairs, the
matched-input control and the controlled operation substitution.

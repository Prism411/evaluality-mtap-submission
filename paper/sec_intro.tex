\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

Video summarization reduces long audiovisual collections to selected keyframes,
shots or segments~\citep{truong2007video_abstraction,apostolidis2021survey}.
When several sources cover the same topic, selection must also handle
cross-source redundancy~\citep{li2010videommr,panda2017collaborative,
wu2020mvsgcn}. A multi-video pipeline therefore combines several criteria: a
segment can be relevant yet redundant, visually informative yet technically
poor, or temporally misplaced.

One stage of such a pipeline scores segments on their visual content, and the field reads
that stage as a quality filter. Colourfulness, contrast, edge distribution, hue count,
sharpness, entropy and saliency area recur there as selection or quality-related
terms~\citep{srinivas2016rank,jeong2016static,qaroush2025diversity,cakmak2025tripss,
huang2024aesthetic,korolkov2025keyframe}, in heterogeneous roles: a stand-alone score for
the frame, a term inside a larger objective, or a threshold that discards. Several
originate elsewhere, in photo-quality classification~\citep{ke2006highlevel},
colourfulness measurement~\citep{hasler2003colorfulness}, retrieval
~\citep{sivic2003videogoogle,csurka2004bovw} and spatial-complexity
characterization~\citep{itu_p910}, each validated against a different question.
\textbf{Treating the individual terms as autonomous, higher-is-better technical-quality
scores is the reuse this article audits.}

The test needs material where the answer is not a matter of opinion. We build 800 pairs
in which the same broadcast content appears before and after a deterministic degradation,
so the member a quality score should rank higher is fixed by construction, and we run
sixteen operations reimplemented from eleven source works through it. \textbf{Every operation whose source declares a direction ranks the degraded copy higher
on at least one degradation type}, and thirteen of the fifteen do so under white noise. For the deployed score the cause is exact: it factors into a descriptor count times a mean
weight, and the count carries the sign of the difference in 797 of the 800 pairs. For the others the article reports a pattern rather than a
mechanism: most are statistics of local structure and additive noise raises local
structure, but two answer white noise correctly and fail elsewhere, so a single cause is
not demonstrated.

Auditing a class needs a system where the criterion is stated rather than implied, and
HSMVideoSumm~\citep{barbieri2021content,barbieri2021tese} is modular enough that the
visual-content operation can be exchanged while every other stage is held fixed. Its
criterion for that stage is human-derived and concerns visual relevance, not image quality;
Section~\ref{sec:related} states it. \textbf{What this article evaluates is the reading the
later literature applies to that stage}, scoring the technical quality of candidate
representatives, and how far a different operation moves the selection.

The scientific issue is the distinction between that criterion and the operation
that implements it. The implementation is a later engineering choice:
Scale-Invariant Feature Transform (SIFT) descriptors are quantized through
Bag-of-Visual-Words (BoVW), weighted with term frequency--inverse document
frequency (TF-IDF), and summed~\citep{lowe2004sift,sivic2003videogoogle,
csurka2004bovw,salton1988tfidf}. SIFT, BoVW and TF-IDF were developed for
matching, retrieval and categorization, not for predicting perceptual quality.
Calling their aggregate a quality score does not establish construct
validity~\citep{cronbach1955construct}, which concerns whether an operational measure
represents the construct attributed to it.

No-reference image quality assessment (NR-IQA) provides alternatives because
segments from different broadcasters have no common undistorted reference. The
available families include natural-scene-statistics, supervised convolutional
and transformer regressors, and vision--language models
~\citep{mittal2012brisque,mittal2013niqe,ke2021musiq,zhang2023liqe,
agnolucci2024qualiclip}. The question is not whether a newer model produces a
higher number, but whether the deployed operation responds to the construct it
names, whether that behavior can be explained, and whether replacing the
operation changes the pipeline output.

This study makes four contributions: a provenance-based inventory of visual-selection
operations and the roles their source works define for them, each fixed by a verbatim
excerpt; a controlled evaluation of degradation response and cross-database generalization,
including a mechanistic analysis of the deployed BoVW+TF-IDF component; an assessment of the
combination rules the sources specify, with our own adaptations identified as such; and an
operation-level substitution study measuring changes in representative selection within a
fixed multi-video pipeline. Training-database overlap,
detectable-keypoint conditioning, alternative pooling, overlay strata,
registered predictions and computational details are diagnostic controls and are reported
in the supplementary material.

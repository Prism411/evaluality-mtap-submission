\title[Do visual-selection heuristics measure technical quality?]{Do Visual-Selection Heuristics Measure Technical Quality? A Controlled Audit of Sixteen Operations Used in Video Summarization}

%% NOTE: confirm author list, ORCIDs and affiliations before submission.
\author*[1]{\fnm{Jader L.} \sur{de Souza Gon\c{c}alves}}\email{jaderlouis@proton.me}
\author[1]{\fnm{Rudinei} \sur{Goularte}}\email{rudinei@icmc.usp.br}
\affil[1]{\orgdiv{Instituto de Ci\^{e}ncias Matem\'{a}ticas e de Computa\c{c}\~{a}o},
  \orgname{Universidade de S\~{a}o Paulo (USP)},
  \orgaddress{\city{S\~{a}o Carlos}, \state{SP}, \country{Brazil}}}

\abstract{
Video summarization systems select segments using visual heuristics such as colourfulness,
contrast and sharpness in heterogeneous roles: some originate in photograph classification,
retrieval or broadcast characterization, others are terms inside summarization objectives.
We audit their reuse as autonomous technical-quality scores. \textbf{Of the sixteen
individual operations, only one is stand-alone in its own source}, and it indexes
colourfulness. We reimplement 16
such heuristics from 11 source works and screen them on 800 pairs in which the same content
appears before and after a known degradation, so the better member is fixed by construction.
\textbf{Thirteen prefer the degraded copy under white noise}, and the Bag-of-Visual-Words
score deployed in a published summarizer inverts on 92\% of those pairs. An exact
factorization explains the failure: the score is a descriptor count times a mean weight, and
the sign of the score difference follows the count difference in 797 of the 800 pairs,
which identifies descriptor-count dominance in the evaluated operation. No heuristic survives a rule
requiring both degradation sensitivity and transfer to unseen databases, and neither do the
nineteen evaluated combinations; three of ten no-reference models do. Substituting an
eligible model into an otherwise fixed pipeline changes 22 of 42 selected segments. No human
judged those segments, so the evidence bears on decision sensitivity rather than perceived
preference. Within that scope, the three eligible no-reference models are defensible replacement
candidates, and \textbf{the quality operation should be reported as a design choice} rather
than an implementation detail.
}

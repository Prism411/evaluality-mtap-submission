# Online Resource 2 — Code, derived data and corpus sample

Article: Do Visual-Selection Operations Track Technical Quality? A Controlled Audit for Multi-Video Summarization
Journal: Multimedia Tools and Applications
Authors: Jader L. de Souza Gonçalves; Rudinei Goularte
Affiliation: Instituto de Ciências Matemáticas e de Computação, Universidade de São Paulo (USP), São Carlos, SP, Brazil
Corresponding author: Jader L. de Souza Gonçalves — jaderlouis@usp.br

Caption: Analysis scripts, derived scores, clusters, seeds, timings and a one-topic, three-video sample supporting the manuscript.

This archive is supplied for single-blind review. Its 313 original entries are unchanged from the earlier submission archive. README_ANONYMIZED.md describes historical path and identity substitutions; it is retained to document those transformations, not as the review policy of this journal. No measurement has been modified. The sample is bebe_real (three videos); the complete corpus is not redistributed. See code_and_data/reproducibility/README.md for dependencies and limits on reproduction. Local paths may require adaptation; public IQA databases and model weights are not bundled.
